Forum Points

Forum Points are how Writer's Forum rewards real engagement. The more your work is genuinely read, liked, saved, and shared, the more Points you earn — and Points are what power everything from getting discovered to joining a Club.

How you earn Points

+2 per Like
+5 per Follow
+1 per completed view
+0.8 per partial view
+10 per Save to library
+15 per Share (by a reader)
+8 per Share (by you, the writer)

Ratings also earn Points

Every star rating your book receives adds Points — higher ratings add a lot more.

What about Dislikes?

Dislikes don't cost you Points directly — but they do lower your book's ranking based on your like-to-dislike ratio. A book with an even split of likes and dislikes will rank much lower than one with mostly likes, even with the same total engagement. Keep readers happy, not just active.

How to spend Points

🚀 Homepage Preview Boost

Feature your book in the homepage spotlight carousel for 1-2 days.

📈 Genre Top Boost

Push your book to the top of its genre listing for 1-2 days.

🏟️ Club Transfers

Clubs spend Points to buy Writers from other Clubs or sign free agents.

New books get a head start

Every new book gets a temporary visibility boost so it has a fair chance to be discovered before it has any engagement yet. This boost fades gradually over 2-4 days as real likes and ratings start counting instead.
